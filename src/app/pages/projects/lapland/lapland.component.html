Vild Expeditions - Lapland Promo Film


Gravel Birds Ultracycling is an unsupported bikepacking challenge in the land without shadows - Alentejo, Portugal. A journey across one of the most remote and striking territories of Southern Europe. Basecamp in Castro Verde. Three distance and routes available: L’Abetarda [350], Gravel Birds Classic [750] or Gravel Birds XL [1000].

Filming the last three editions of Gravel Birds has been an incredible experience. Capturing riders as they push through the open plains and dusty gravel roads of the Alentejo showed me the true spirit of bikepacking. Between the long days under the Portuguese sun and the strong sense of community among the cyclists, documenting this raw and beautiful event was something truly special.
