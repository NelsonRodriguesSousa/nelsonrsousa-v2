Soajo Outdoor Fest 2026 - Aftermovie


Outdoor Fest is a festival that connects outdoor activities, music, and gastronomy. It originated in the village of Soajo, in the heart of the Peneda-Gerês National Park, where it is based.

A mountain village marked by its rural character. Since its foundation, our main objective has been the fusion between ancestral rural cultures and the future.

For more than a week, I had the chance to document Outdoor Fest in Soajo, capturing adventure, outdoor activities, music, gastronomy, and the unique spirit of the village.

It was an intense week of early mornings, long days, trails, unpredictable moments, and endless stories to capture. More than filming a festival, it was about experiencing the connection between nature, people, culture, and adventure.
